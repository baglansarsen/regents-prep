Privacy Policy

Regentify — NY Regents Exam Prep · Last updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Regentify mobile app (“Regentify,” “we,” “us,” or “the app”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in (including with Apple or Google), we collect your email address and display name to create and secure your account.
  • Study and activity data. Your quiz results, progress, reward points (RP), streaks, achievements, virtual pet, friends, battle challenges, and leaderboard standings, so the app can save your progress and power its features.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA). If you grant permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, your device’s advertising identifier may be used to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.
  • Technical data. Basic, standard diagnostic information needed to keep the app running reliably.

How we use your information

  • To provide and save your study progress and gameplay features.
  • To enable social features (friends, leaderboards, and battle challenges).
  • To display advertising that helps keep the app free for students.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the service.

Third-party services

We rely on the following service providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — account sign-in and storage of your study/activity data. Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising. Google Privacy

Tracking

The app uses Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. We do not access your advertising identifier or use it for personalized advertising unless you grant permission when prompted. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Children’s privacy

Regentify is an educational study tool intended for high-school students. We do not knowingly collect more personal information than is necessary to provide the service. If you believe a child has provided information you would like removed, contact us at the address below.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from within the app, or by emailing us. Once deleted, your study data and account information are removed from our systems.

Your choices

  • Decline ad tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt or device Settings.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
