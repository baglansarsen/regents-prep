Privacy Policy for Regentify

Last updated: June 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SBT Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Regentify mobile application (the “App”), a study app for New York State Regents exam preparation. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account information Email address, password (handled by our authentication provider), or your Google / Apple ID when you sign in with those services. To create and secure your account and sign you in.
Profile information Display name, profile photo (optional), school, and grade level. To personalize your experience and show relevant content.
Location information Approximate or precise device location (only while using the App, with your permission). To help you find schools near your current location.
Usage & progress data Quizzes completed, scores, experience points, streaks, and in-app activity. To save your progress and provide study features.
Purchase information Subscription status and transaction identifiers (processed by Apple and our subscription provider). To manage subscriptions and unlock premium features.
Advertising & device data Advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your consent), device type, and push notification token. To show ads and (with your permission) send notifications.

We do not knowingly collect more information than is needed to provide the App, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App and its study features;
  • To create and secure your account and authenticate you;
  • To save and sync your study progress across sessions and devices;
  • To process subscriptions and provide premium content;
  • To display advertising that helps keep the App free;
  • To send notifications you have opted into;
  • To respond to your support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. Advertising and Tracking

The App displays advertising through Google AdMob. On iOS, before any advertising identifier is used, the App presents Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt asking for your permission. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time in your device’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following service providers, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (authentication & cloud database) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple & App Store purchases) — apple.com/legal/privacy

These providers process data on our behalf and may receive information as needed to perform their services.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only with the service providers listed above, when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the App. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below, and we will delete it within a reasonable period except where retention is required by law. If account deletion is available within the App, you may also delete your account directly from the app settings.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and access controls provided by our infrastructure providers, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

Regentify is intended for high-school students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information, including rights under the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by other appropriate means.
