01 · The Mark

Meet Reggie.

Reggie is a round, friendly baby dinosaur wearing a graduation cap — the face of learning that levels up. The mark always sits inside a rounded green tile, balanced by XP-yellow spikes, feet and tassel.

1
Squircle tile
A 118px-radius rounded square in the brand green gradient — the constant container for the mark.
2
XP accents
Back-spikes, feet and cap tassel in XP Yellow — the reward color carried from the product.
3
Graduation cap
Ink-black mortarboard signals education; it anchors the silhouette and reads even at small sizes.
4
Belly & eye
Mint belly and a single oversized eye give Reggie warmth and a clear focal point.

Logo BookConstruction

03 · Construction

Built on a grid.

The tile is a 512-unit square with a 16-unit margin and 118-unit corner radius. Reggie is centered on the optical axis; the cap and eye align to the upper third.

Grid · 512 units
16
Tile margin
16 units of breathing room inside the 512 artboard keeps the squircle off every edge.
118
Corner radius
A fixed 118-unit radius — the same squircle softness used across all product surfaces.
⌖
Optical centering
Reggie shifts slightly right of true center so the back-spikes don’t pull the silhouette off balance.
Never rebuild the mark by hand — always use the supplied vector files04

05 · Color

Two tiles, one palette.

Use the green gradient tile on light and neutral surfaces. On dark or busy backgrounds, use the solid Spark Green tile so the squircle keeps a clean edge. The mark inside never changes proportion or color.

Logo BookMisuse

06 · Don’ts

Protect the mark.

The mark is a fixed asset. Don’t alter its color, proportion, orientation or effects — these examples are all incorrect.

✕
Don’t stretch or distort the proportions.
✕
Don’t recolor the tile to off-brand hues.
✕
Don’t rotate or tilt the mark.
✕
Don’t add drop shadows or effects.
✕
Don’t place the tile-less mark on a busy bg.
✕
Don’t desaturate or convert to mono.
When in doubt, use a supplied file unchanged07
