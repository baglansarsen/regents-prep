Reggie is the Regentify mascot — use him at emotional moments (welcome, correct-streak celebration, hints, streak-at-risk reminders), never as decoration on dense screens.

```jsx
<Reggie pose="cheer" size={180} />
```

Poses: `happy` (idle/default), `cheer` (celebrate a win — arms up, sparkles), `think` (hints/explanations — "?" bubble), `sleepy` (streak about to break — zzz), `wave` (greeting/login). Always transparent background; place on mint or white. Keep him at least 96px so the cap reads.
