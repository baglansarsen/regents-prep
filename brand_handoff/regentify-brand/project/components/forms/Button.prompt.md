The signature chunky Regentify button — green "pop" with a solid 5px bottom shadow that presses down on tap. One primary action per screen.

```jsx
<Button onClick={next}>Continue</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Skip</Button>
<Button variant="pro" icon="⭐">Go Pro</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (green), `secondary` (white outline), `pro` (violet), `danger` (coral). Sizes `sm|md|lg`, plus `full` and `icon`.
