Logo concepts — Round 1

Three directions for the app mark. The primary idea is a graduation cap that doubles as a “level-up” arrow; concepts 1 & 2 explore two takes on that, and concept 3 brings in Reggie the owl as a mascot alternate. Every mark is built as layered SVG on the brand green gradient. Tell me which to refine.

Summit

Primary direction

The mortarboard and the up-arrow are the same shape — one bold white arrow, its wide head reading as a cap board, with an XP-yellow tassel swinging off the top. Maximum momentum, minimum parts. Reads cleanly all the way down to a favicon.
