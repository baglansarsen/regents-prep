Usage & palette

Clear space & minimum size

Give the full-body character clear space of at least ¼ his height. Use the free-standing mascot at 96px and up; below that, switch to the circular face avatar. Never crop the cap or feet on the full body.

One character, many moods

Keep proportions, cap, eye and belly fixed — only the arms, mouth and eye change between poses. Don’t recolor Reggie, add outlines, rotate him, or place him on a busy photo. On dark surfaces the gradient body still reads; no tile needed.

SVG exports — assets/reggie/

Each pose is exported as a standalone, transparent SVG (full body, no tile). Drop them straight into screens — heroes, empty states, toasts, celebrations. Generated from a single source, assets/reggie/reggie-character.js, so poses stay perfectly consistent. For the circular avatar, render any pose inside a border-radius:50% mask on a brand-color fill.
