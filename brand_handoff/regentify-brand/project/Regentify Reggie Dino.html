Usage & palette

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space of at least ¼ the tile width on all sides. Minimum app-icon size 24px; below 32px switch to the simplified mark (no spikes, feet or tassel — just body, eye, snout & cap).

Gradient vs solid tile

Use the green gradient tile on light or neutral surfaces. On dark or busy backgrounds, use the solid Spark Green tile so the squircle keeps a clean edge. The mark inside never changes proportion.
