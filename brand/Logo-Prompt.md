# Regentify — Logo Design Prompt (Duolingo-playful)

Paste this into Claude (or any image/design model). It's aligned to the Regentify brand book.

---

**Design a playful app logo for "Regentify."**

Regentify is a gamified NY Regents Exam prep app for high school students — think *Duolingo for state exams*. The vibe is bright, bouncy, and encouraging, but still trustworthy because real grades are on the line. The name plays on **Regents** (the exam) + **-ify** (the playful, gamified spin).

**Style — lean fully Duolingo-playful:**
- Friendly, rounded, chunky shapes. Soft geometry, thick strokes, no sharp corners.
- Flat with a subtle top-to-bottom gradient on the icon. No skeuomorphism, no fine detail, no drop shadows.
- Cheerful and energetic — like a reward, not a textbook.

**Icon mark:**
- A rounded **squircle** app-icon tile (iOS-style ~24px corner radius).
- Inside: a simple **graduation cap that doubles as an upward "level-up" arrow** — signaling growth, achievement, momentum. Keep it bold and instantly readable.
- Must stay clear at small sizes; design so it still reads as an app icon at 32px and a favicon at 16–24px (drop the arrow tail at the smallest sizes, keep the cap silhouette).

**Wordmark:**
- "Regentify" in a **rounded geometric sans** (Fredoka / Baloo / Nunito feel), heavy weight.
- Split the color: **"Regent"** in deep ink/green, **"-ify"** in bright XP yellow to highlight the playful twist.
- Provide a horizontal lockup (icon left of wordmark) and a stacked lockup (icon above wordmark).

**Color palette (use these exact values):**
- Spark Green `#1FC36B` — primary
- Forest `#0E9F52` — gradient depth / pressed
- XP Yellow `#FFC93C` — the "-ify" accent
- Ink `#0F2018` — dark text
- Mint Wash `#EAFBF1` — light background
- Icon: green gradient (`#1FC36B → #0E9F52`) on light backgrounds; solid Spark Green on dark.

**Optional mascot tie-in:**
- The brand mascot is **Reggie**, a wise-but-goofy owl in a graduation cap (green feathers, mint belly, XP-yellow beak/feet). A mascot version of the icon (just Reggie's head in the squircle) would be a nice alternate — show it as a secondary option, not the primary mark.

**Deliverables:**
1. The icon mark on its own (on light and on dark).
2. Horizontal lockup + stacked lockup.
3. An app-icon mockup on a rounded square home screen.
4. A favicon-size test (16–32px) proving it stays legible.
5. Show **2–3 distinct concepts** first, then refine the strongest one.
6. Final output as clean, layered **SVG**.

After the first round, I'll tell you which concept to refine.
