02 · Logo & Mark

The mark

A rounded squircle app icon holding a graduation cap that doubles as an upward "level-up" arrow — growth, achievement, momentum. The wordmark splits Regent (the exam) and -ify (the playful spin) with a pop of XP yellow.

Primary lockup — light

Reverse lockup — dark

Regentify

App icon · scale test (read clearly down to a favicon)

Below ~24px, drop the arrow tail and keep only the cap silhouette.

✓ Do

  • Keep clear space ≥ the height of the cap around the lockup.
  • Use the gradient icon on light backgrounds, solid green on dark.
  • Keep "-ify" in XP yellow whenever color is available.

✕ Don't

  • Recolor the wordmark outside the brand palette.
  • Stretch, rotate, or add drop shadows to the mark.
  • Place the icon on a busy photo without a solid backing.

05 · Mascot

Meet Reggie

A wise-but-goofy little owl in a graduation cap — Regentify's cheerleader, study buddy, and the face of every streak reminder. Reggie is encouraging, never nagging; proud of you, never disappointed.

✓ Reggie is

  • Warm, hype, and a bit silly
  • Always on the student's side
  • Celebrating small wins loudly

✕ Reggie isn't

  • Strict, scolding, or guilt-tripping
  • A know-it-all professor
  • Babyish or overly cutesy

This is a reference sketch to define proportions, palette, and vibe. Final art should be a polished vector character with expression variants (happy, cheering, sleepy/streak-at-risk, celebrating).

06 · Voice & Tone

Talk like a hype-friend who happens to know the answer key.

Short, encouraging, second-person. Celebrate effort. Make mistakes feel safe. Keep academic accuracy non-negotiable.

✓ Say this

  • "Nice — 4 in a row! Keep the streak alive. 🔥"
  • "Close! Let's break this one down together."
  • "You just unlocked Geometry. Let's go."

✕ Not this

  • "Incorrect. Please review the material."
  • "You have failed to maintain your streak."
  • "Mastery of this competency is required."

Tone dials

Playful but credible · Encouraging but honest · Casual but clear · Brief but warm.
