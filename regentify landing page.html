FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Regentify free?
Yes. Regentify is free to download and use for every NY student — all 11 subjects and 200+ authentic NYSED exams included. An optional Premium subscription adds Unlimited Hearts and removes ads.
What subjects does Regentify cover?
All 11 NY Regents subjects: Living Environment, Earth Science, Chemistry, Physics, Life Science, Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, English (ELA), Global History & Geography, and US History & Government.
Are these real Regents exams?
Yes — Regentify uses authentic, past NYSED Regents exams, so you practice with the exact question style, format, and difficulty of the real test.
Does it help for both the January and June Regents?
Absolutely. The content covers every administration, so you can prep for the January, June, or August Regents with daily practice, streaks, and a predicted score.
How much does Premium cost?
The core app stays free. Premium is optional: $4.99/month, $9.99 for a 3-month Season Pass, or $24.99/year — for Unlimited Hearts and an ad-free experience.
What devices can I use Regentify on?
Regentify is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Download it free and start with a quick placement test to focus on what you need most.
