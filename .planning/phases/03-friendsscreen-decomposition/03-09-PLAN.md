<objective>
Split the Friends tab container into named widgets: the friend-code banner with its copy and add controls, and the friend card with its tap, long-press and two action controls.

**As a** student sharing my code and managing friends, **I want to** copy my code, add someone, tap into a profile, long-press to remove, and start a battle exactly as before, **so that** the Friends tab being broken into widgets is invisible to me.

Purpose: the friend card carries the screen's only long-press affordance and the only place where two different handlers sit on one pressable. Giving it its own file and its own interaction test is what makes that wiring provable rather than assumed.

Output: `FriendCodeBanner.jsx`, `FriendCard.jsx`, two tests, and a `FriendsTab` reduced to composition.
</objective>
