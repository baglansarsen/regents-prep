<objective>
Extract the three always-visible chrome pieces that sit above the tab content — the pending friend-request banner, the incoming-battle banner, and the four-tab bar — into named components, and bring `mobile/src/screens/FriendsScreen.jsx` to its final form: one orchestration hook call, a little shell markup, and four container renders. This is the plan that closes FRIENDS-02.

**As a** student opening the Social screen, **I want to** still see pending friend requests and battle challenges the moment the screen opens, and still switch tabs the same way, **so that** the last of the screen's markup moving into components is invisible to me.

Purpose: FRIENDS-02 asks for a screen reduced to calling a hook and rendering. Everything except this chrome is already out. After this plan the screen can be gated mechanically for the absence of state, effects, dialogs and direct routing calls.

Output: `FriendRequestBanner.jsx`, `IncomingBattleBanner.jsx`, `FriendsTabBar.jsx`, three interaction tests, and the final screen.
