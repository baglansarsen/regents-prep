<objective>
Move the Friends tab and the Battles tab out of `mobile/src/screens/FriendsScreen.jsx` into two named containers under `mobile/src/components/FriendsScreen/`, driven entirely by the orchestration hook's flat return. After this plan the screen holds no tab-body JSX at all.

**As a** student managing friendships and battles, **I want to** copy my friend code, add a friend, tap into a profile, long-press to remove someone, start a battle, play a pending challenge and ask for a rematch exactly as before, **so that** two more branches moving into their own files is invisible to me.

Purpose: these are the last two tab bodies still inline in the screen. Extracting both in one plan is what keeps the screen-editing chain short — after this only the shell chrome remains, and plans 03-09 and 03-10 can split these two containers into widgets in parallel without touching the screen again.

Output: `FriendsTab.jsx`, `BattlesTab.jsx`, two interaction tests, and a screen whose tab switch is four container renders.
</objective>
