<objective>
Move the whole leaderboard tab — the friends/school/league segment control and all three mode bodies — out of `mobile/src/screens/FriendsScreen.jsx` and into one named container under `mobile/src/components/FriendsScreen/`, driven entirely by the orchestration hook's flat return.

**As a** student checking where I rank, **I want to** switch between my friends, my school and my league and see exactly the podium, rows, prompts and footers I saw before, **so that** the largest branch of this screen moving into its own file is invisible to me.

Purpose: this is the biggest and most branch-dense of the four tabs, and the only one that composes the three animated components relocated in plan 03-02. Extracting it whole, with its JSX still inline, is the step that lets plans 03-07 and 03-08 split it into widgets without ever touching the screen file again.

Output: `LeaderboardTab.jsx`, its interaction test, and a screen whose leaderboard branch is one line.
</objective>
