Split the Battles tab container into its two named row components, keeping them deliberately separate and leaving the per-battle derivation in the container.

Purpose: these two rows look superficially similar and are the exact case Phase 2's extraction template flagged as a unification trap. Splitting them into two files with two tests makes the difference structural instead of a comment.

Output: `PendingBattleRow.jsx`, `CompletedBattleRow.jsx`, two tests, and a `BattlesTab` reduced to sections plus derivation.
</objective>
