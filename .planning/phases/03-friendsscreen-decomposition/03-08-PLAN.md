<objective>
Split the leaderboard container's remaining two mode bodies into named widgets: the school-mode body with its prompt and its ranked row, and the league-mode body with its banner and its ranked row. After this plan `LeaderboardTab` composes five widgets and holds no mode markup of its own.

**As a** student checking my school's and my league's standings, **I want to** see the same prompts, rows, medals and footers as before, **so that** the last of the leaderboard being broken into widgets is invisible to me.

Purpose: this is pass two of the leaderboard split, in its own wave because both passes edit the same container file. It also captures the one genuinely different behavior between the two ranked lists — the self-word substitution — as a structural fact rather than a hidden flag.

Output: five components, five tests, and a `LeaderboardTab` reduced to composition.
</objective>
