# Parked Ideas

## TAM Expansion — AP Exams & Beyond
**Status:** Discussed, parked for later

### Key decisions to make
- Prioritize AP exams first (same audience, same pipeline, national TAM ~5M students)
- Content pipeline (shared/content/ PDF crawler) already works — expansion is a content problem not a product problem
- Product needs: exam selection at onboarding, per-exam progress tracking, multi-namespace content routing via @content
- Brand question: does "Regentify" flex to AP, or do we need a sub-brand / rename?

### Sequencing
1. AP (lowest effort, highest overlap with current users)
2. Other state exit exams (STAAR, FAST, CAASPP) — risk: content maintenance fragmentation
3. SAT/ACT — highest TAM but crowded; gamification stack is the differentiator

### Why AP first
- NY students already take AP exams — same user, zero new acquisition cost
- Turns a seasonal product (Jan–June Regents) into year-round → higher LTV
- College Board publishes free released exams going back years → same PDF pipeline

### Open questions
- How much engineering effort for multi-exam product support?
- Content ops system vs. manual per-exam builds?
- Pricing/monetization for AP tier vs. Regents tier?
